# 1. Vision Statement

> WiseEvidence aims to make the global body of homeopathy research easier to discover, understand, compare, and critically evaluate through a structured, transparent, AI-assisted and human-curated evidence platform.

# 2. The Problem

Homeopathy research is scattered across:

- Journals
- PubMed
- Scholarly metadata services
- Government repositories
- Institutional repositories
- Homeopathy-specific organizations
- Research databases
- Journal websites

Finding a paper does not necessarily answer:

- What type of research is it?
- What condition was studied?
- What intervention was studied?
- What did the researchers actually find?
- Was the result positive, negative, mixed, or inconclusive?
- How strong is the evidence?
- What limitations were reported?
- Has the study been criticized?
- Where is the original publication?
- What is the DOI?

WiseEvidence is intended to reduce this fragmentation.

# 3. Core Idea

```text
Scattered Research
→ Discovery
→ Normalization
→ Deduplication
→ Structured Metadata
→ AI Assistance
→ Human Review
→ Evidence Record
→ Research Explorer
```

# 4. What WiseEvidence Is

- Research discovery platform
- Structured research repository
- Evidence exploration system
- Research visualization platform
- AI-assisted research indexing system
- Human-curated evidence library
- Open-source research infrastructure project

# 5. What WiseEvidence Is Not

- Replacement for peer review
- Medical treatment recommendation engine
- Diagnostic system
- Substitute for original research
- Automated scientific truth engine
- Researcher reputation-ranking platform
- System that hides contradictory evidence

# 6. Mission

> Make homeopathy research easier to find and easier to understand while preserving the distinction between reported findings, evidence quality, criticism, uncertainty, and interpretation.

# 7. Long-Term Vision

WiseEvidence should become an interactive research evidence atlas.

Users should eventually be able to navigate:

```text
Homeopathy
→ Condition
→ Research
→ Evidence Level
→ Outcome
→ Quality
→ Criticism
```

or:

```text
Research Type
→ Condition
→ Intervention
→ Study
→ Outcome
```

# 8. Three-Layer Evidence Model

## Layer 1
What kind of research exists?

Evidence Pyramid.

## Layer 2
What did the research report?

Outcome Spectrum.

## Layer 3
How strong or reliable is the evidence?

Evidence Quality / Confidence.

# 9. Critical Distinctions

The platform must distinguish:

- Study Outcome
- Evidence Quality
- Criticism
- Confidence
- Provenance

# 10. AI Role

AI reduces repetitive work.

It may:

- Extract metadata
- Summarize
- Identify characteristics
- Suggest classifications
- Extract conditions/interventions
- Generate keywords
- Detect possible duplicates
- Identify related research

AI is not the final authority.

# 11. Human Role

Humans verify important classifications.

```text
AI
→ Suggestion
→ Human
→ Verification
→ Publication
```

# 12. Transparency

Users should eventually be able to understand:

- Where a record came from
- What the paper reported
- What AI generated
- What a human reviewer changed
- Why a classification was assigned
- When it was reviewed

# 13. Target Users

- Homeopathy researchers
- Practicing doctors
- Students
- Academic institutions
- Evidence reviewers
- General readers
- Developers and contributors

# 14. User Promise

> Find the research. Understand what it studied. See what it reported. Understand its limitations. Follow the original source.

# 15. Open Source Vision

Researchers and developers should eventually be able to contribute:

- Research records
- Metadata corrections
- Taxonomy improvements
- Reviews
- Classification suggestions
- Documentation
- Code
- Importers
- Visualizations
- AI integrations

# 16. Product Principles

- Discoverability
- Transparency
- Traceability
- Structured neutrality
- Human oversight
- Simplicity
- Cost efficiency
- Open contribution
- Reversibility

# 17. Vision Boundary

The first version should focus on building the best structured research discovery experience possible before attempting a fully autonomous evidence intelligence system.

# 18. Final Vision

WiseEvidence should make the fragmented homeopathy research landscape an understandable, navigable and transparent evidence map.

It should help users inspect evidence themselves rather than asking them to trust a single conclusion.

**Find it. Understand it. Compare it. Question it. Follow the source. Explore the evidence.**
