  state without changing anything.

  14-step, laptop-friendly, secret-safe walkthrough of the live gates.
- **M7 readiness review** (`docs/reports/M7-READINESS-REVIEW.md`): documents the
  existing ingestion substrate and the genuine prerequisites/questions for a future
